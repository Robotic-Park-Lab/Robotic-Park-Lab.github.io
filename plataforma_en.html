Infrastructure

Robotic Park

Robotic Park is the lab's multi-agent experimental platform: a heterogeneous fleet of real robots synchronised with their simulated counterparts (hardware-in-the-loop), designed both for research in distributed control and for hands-on robotics teaching with ROS 2.

New

Build your own experiment

Configure an experiment on Robotic Park from the browser: number and type of robots (Crazyflie 2.1, Khepera IV), real, virtual or digital twin, low-level controllers and event-based communication, formation shape and topology, control law (gradient, Lagrange multipliers, herding, leader-based mesh), Webots world and the commands sent to each agent over time. The page generates the experience, supervisor script, Webots world and RViz files, plus install.sh and run.sh to run it directly on a ROS 2 computer like the lab's.

Hardware

Agents and sensing

Crazyflie 2.X

Open-source quadrotors for aerial formation and event-based control experiments.

Khepera IV

Differential ground robots, with a hardware-in-the-loop simulator in ROS/Gazebo/Matlab.

LIMO

Multi-sensor ground robotic platform (Ackermann/differential) integrated in ROS 2.

DJI Tello

Micro drones for rapid prototyping, with the lab's own ROS 2 driver.

Vicon motion capture system

High-accuracy localisation for all the lab's real agents, integrated in ROS 2.

Vision (Orbbec)

Orbbec depth cameras for perception and obstacle detection, with the lab's own ROS 2 wrapper.

Software

Repositories

All the lab's software is open and published at github.com/Robotic-Park-Lab.

RoboticPark

Main repository of the experimental platform and its benchmarks.

uned_crazyflie_ros_pkg

Hardware-in-the-loop simulator of the Crazyflie 2.1 drone in ROS, Gazebo and Matlab.

uned_kheperaIV_ros_pkg

Hardware-in-the-loop simulator of the Khepera IV differential robot in ROS, Gazebo and Matlab.

uned_limo_ros_pkg

ROS integration of the LIMO platform.

uned_multi_agent_ros_pkg

Multi-agent coordination for heterogeneous combinations of the lab's platforms.

uned_vision_ros_pkg

Perception and computer vision for the platform's agents.

ros2-vicon-receiver

ROS 2 receiver for the Vicon motion capture system.

uned_tello_ros_pkg

ROS 2 packages for DJI Tello drones, with the original driver as a submodule.

OrbbecSDK_ROS2

ROS 2 wrapper of the Orbbec depth camera SDK.

crazyflie-firmware

Fork of the Crazyflie Nano/Bolt Quadcopter firmware used in the lab.

crazyflie-lib-python

Python library for communicating with the Crazyflie.
