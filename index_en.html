Control and robotics of Multi-Agent Robotic Systems

We design, control and evaluate heterogeneous robot fleets — drones, ground robots and virtual agents — combining distributed control, cyber-physical systems and mixed reality on a single open experimental platform: Robotic Park.

Research lines

What we work on

Multi-robot formation control

Distance-based formations and virtual surface constraints, event-based control and consensus protocols for heterogeneous fleets.

Resilient cyber-physical systems

Distributed control architectures that are secure against threats and attacks in cooperative cyber-physical systems.

Mixed reality for swarm robotics

Real and virtual agents combined in the same extended experimental environment to scale up swarm experiments.

Aerial robotics (UAV) and Crazyflie drones

Identification, calibration and control of Crazyflie 2.X quadrotors for multi-agent experimentation.

Agricultural robotics in greenhouses

Autonomous navigation and hierarchical control of mobile robots in Mediterranean greenhouse environments.

Experimental teaching in control and robotics

Robotic Park as a teaching platform: reproducible benchmarks and open lab sessions for teaching control and robotics with ROS 2.

The platform

Robotic Park

Robotic Park is the lab's multi-agent experimental platform: it integrates Crazyflie drones, ground robots (Khepera IV, LIMO), Vicon motion capture and a hardware-in-the-loop bridge between real and simulated agents, all on ROS 2. It is both research infrastructure and a teaching resource.
