Benchmarks

We publish open benchmarks to objectively compare multi-robot control and navigation algorithms on Robotic Park. Each benchmark defines an objective and a reference dataset recorded in ROS 2 (ros2 bag) that you can download to develop and evaluate your own solution.

How to take part? Run your method on the benchmark dataset, compute the stated metric and send your result (method, metric obtained and, if possible, a link to your code) to lab.nanodrones@dia.uned.es with the benchmark name in the subject. We will add it to the corresponding ranking.

Multi-robot formation control

Description

Compares formation controllers (distance-based, consensus or virtual surface constraints) on a heterogeneous Robotic Park fleet, under exactly the same initial conditions, desired topology and disturbances.

Objective

Reach and hold the target formation while minimising the steady-state formation error and the convergence time, with a topology change halfway through the test.

Data

  • Format: rosbag2 (SQLite3 / MCAP)
  • Agents: 4 ground robots (Khepera IV / LIMO)
  • Topics: /robotN/pose (Vicon), /robotN/cmd_vel, /tf
  • Rate: 50 Hz (Vicon), 20 Hz (control)
  • Episode length: ~90 s
  • Scenarios: line formation, square formation and dynamic reconfiguration

Hierarchical navigation in a greenhouse

Description

Evaluates hierarchical control strategies for the autonomous navigation of a transport mobile robot in a Mediterranean greenhouse, with narrow aisles and changing lighting conditions.

Objective

Complete a point-to-point delivery route while minimising the total time and the number of interventions/recoveries, without colliding with the greenhouse structure.

Data

  • Format: rosbag2 (MCAP)
  • Agent: differential/Ackermann transport robot
  • Topics: /scan, /odom, /tf, /cmd_vel, /map
  • Scenario: real and simulated (MVSim) greenhouse map
